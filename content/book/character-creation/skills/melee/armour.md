# Melee weapon skills

## Armour skill

![Armour](/images/Skills/Melee/Armour.png)
_This is your armour. Treat it well, and it will protect you. Treat it poorly, and it will hang limp from your body. Any young recruit must learn how to wear armour, treat armour, and especially, how to use armour. Do you know how to roll with a blow? Do you know how to place your feet when someone is forcing down a sledgehammer on your arm? Do you know how to hold your ground? If the answer to these questions is ‘no,’ then I suggest you learn. And I suggest you learn quickly, because a dead recruit is also a very quiet one.”_
-Gerrin Tergshire, Lieutenant of the Ceygn City Guard.

The Armour skill represents a character’s proficiency with wearing and using armour. Armour can be cumbersome, and using it to remain safe from harm is a skill, one that relatively few people possess.
While most civilians might think all there is to armour is donning the heavy, protective layers, a seasoned warrior knows better than that: Using armour well requires its own finesse.

Armour price listings can be found here.
Characters can make an Armour Defense Check against attacks dealing Physical damage. If you are hit by a Physical attack, you may roll a die, then add your Armour skill rank to the check result. If you score 10 or higher, you negate from that attack an amount of physical damage equal to your Armour’s maximum Absorb value or your [Fit] score, whichever is lower. There are certain character options that can raise the absorb value above that which a character’s [Fit] score would normally allow. These options include the skilled use of a shield, enchantments, special equipment, or the use of the Protection skill.

Wearing armour also imposes penalties. Each rank of the Armour skill reduces those penalties by 1.

The Armour skill does not involve using any hand-held weapons; as such, it does not grant the Warrior special ability like ordinary Melee Weapon Skills would.
